How it works

Simple for residents. Simple for management.

CrestHaven becomes the bridge between each apartment door and the property's existing dumpster.

STEP 01

Residents place waste outside

Residents follow the property's designated collection window and place their bagged waste in the provided household bin.

STEP 02

CrestHaven collects

Our collector makes the scheduled route through the property and retrieves the waste from each participating unit.

STEP 03

Waste goes to the dumpster

Collected bags are moved to the building's existing central waste area for normal disposal.
